## How Eco Happy started

Araatrika started in 2017 with a small exhibition stall of handcrafted recycled products, made
by our founder, Ar. Jagruti Khabiya Jain. It's grown into a team of eight women (and counting)
working out of a workshop in Parvati Paytha, Pune, hand-rolling newspaper into seed pencils and
making seed paper, upcycled-fabric and plantable festive products.

## What we actually do

We make plastic-free, plantable stationery and gifts, seed pencils, seed paper, plantable
rakhis and kites, upcycled diaries and bags, by hand, in Pune. Every product is designed so the
part you're done with can go back into soil instead of a bin. We keep our claims specific: if we
say something is plastic-free or plantable, that's true of the actual product you'll receive, not
a marketing line.

## Meet the founder

Ar. Jagruti Khabiya Jain is an architect (Savitribai Phule Pune University) who worked in
Auroville and Delhi before founding Araatrika in Pune. She leads the team that designs, makes and
ships everything on this site.
