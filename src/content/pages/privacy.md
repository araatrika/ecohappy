*Last updated: 25 October 2026*

Eco Happy is a brand of Araatrika, a small business based in Pune, India. This notice explains which personal details we collect when you use ecohappy.in, why we need them, who else handles them, and what you can ask us to do. Please read it together with our [Terms & conditions](/terms/).



## 1. What we collect

When you place an order, we collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, city and pincode. If you ask for a GST invoice, we also collect your GSTIN.

When you send an enquiry through a form on this site, we collect the name, email address and phone number you enter, along with the message you write.

When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, we see what you send, including the name and number shown on your WhatsApp profile.

Our hosting provider keeps standard server records, such as the time of a visit and the page requested, so that the website stays up and secure.

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this website.



## 2. Why we use your details

- To take your payment, process your order and deliver it to you.
- To send you your receipt or GST invoice, and to contact you about your delivery.
- To answer your enquiries, including quotes for bulk, corporate and custom orders.
- To keep the records that Indian tax and accounting rules require us to keep.
- To keep the website working and to stop spam and misuse.

We do not sell your personal details.



## 3. Who else handles your details

**Razorpay** processes your payment. When you pay, Razorpay receives your name, email address, phone number and, if you gave one, your GSTIN, so that the payment is linked to your order. Card, UPI and net banking details are entered directly into Razorpay's checkout. We never see or store your card details.

**Netlify** hosts this website and runs our order and enquiry forms. After a payment is confirmed, the order details (name, contact details, delivery address, product, amount and payment reference) are saved to an order form in our Netlify account. Enquiry form messages are saved there too.

**Zoho Mail** hosts our email inbox. Emails you send us are stored there.

**WhatsApp** is operated by Meta. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Links on this site to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Google reviews and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.



## 4. How long we keep them

We keep order and invoice records for as long as Indian tax and accounting rules require. We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to answer you and to keep our records, and then delete them.



## 5. Your choices

You can ask us to:

- tell you which personal details we hold about you,
- correct them,
- delete them, except records we must keep for tax and accounting, or
- stop contacting you about an enquiry.

Write to us at contact@happypencils.in, or message us on [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/919422062333). If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.



## 6. Keeping your details safe

The website and our forms use secure (HTTPS) connections. Payments are handled by Razorpay. We limit access to order and enquiry details to the people who run Eco Happy and need them to serve you. No online system is perfectly secure, so if something goes wrong with your details, we will tell you and take the steps the law asks of us.



## 7. Cookies and browser storage

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. After you pay, the payment result is kept in your browser's session storage for that tab only, and is cleared when you close the tab. Razorpay may set its own cookies while its checkout is open, under its own policy.



## 8. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices change. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
