We've supplied sustainable stationery to Six Senses Hotels Resorts Spas for guest amenities across several of their properties, including Six Senses Zighy Bay, Six Senses Con Dao, and Six Senses Krabey Island.

It's a specific kind of brief to work on. Luxury hospitality can't compromise on how something looks or feels in a guest's hands, and it also can't claim to be eco-conscious while handing out plastic pens at check-in. Plantable and recycled-paper stationery solves both problems at once: it reads as premium, and it's genuinely lower-impact than what it replaces.
